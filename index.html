Frequently asked
questions

How much should we budget for this?

Pricing depends entirely on scope and complexity, so we quote each project individually. The best way to get an accurate number is a quick call where we talk through what you need.

How fast can you deliver?

Most single deliverables land within one to two weeks. Ongoing content retainers run on a fixed weekly cadence agreed up front, so you always know what is arriving and when.

What if we need changes?

Every project includes two rounds of revisions at each stage. We review direction before production starts, which is where most changes get caught early and cheaply.

What platforms do you deliver for?

Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube, and LinkedIn as standard, plus paid ad formats and web. Every asset is exported in the right ratio and length for its platform.

Why not just hire a general agency?

General agencies learn your market on your budget. We already work inside Web3, trading, and fintech every day, so the strategy conversation starts at depth instead of at definitions.

Do you just animate, or do you handle the full creative?

Full creative. Strategy, concept, copy, design, motion, and distribution. Animation is one step in a system, not the product itself.

Can you become our ongoing content team?

Yes. Most of our client relationships run as monthly retainers where we function as the embedded creative team, from planning through publishing.
