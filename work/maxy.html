Making Bitcoin's hardest tech visible.

For almost a year, we've been Maxy's creative partner, animating the cryptography behind the protocol and helping prepare the company for launch.

Overview

Maxy Protocol is built on one uncompromising idea: Bitcoin never leaves mainnet. Cross-chain swaps secured by cryptographic proof instead of custodians. No wrapped assets, no middlemen, no trust required. It's one of the most technically demanding propositions in Web3, and that's exactly the problem. The better the engineering, the harder it is to show.

That's where we come in. As Maxy's creative partner, we translate what the protocol actually does, the code, the verification, the proofs, into animation that anyone can follow. What started with website animations grew into a partnership that now spans the entire brand.

The Challenge

This is the most technical brief in our portfolio. Maxy doesn't sell a lifestyle, it sells cryptographic certainty. Every animation has to be correct before it can be beautiful. How verification works. What the smart contracts actually execute. Why Bitcoin staying on mainnet changes the risk model entirely. Get one detail wrong and the animation doesn't simplify the technology, it misrepresents it.

Most creative teams would need months of onboarding just to hold that conversation. We're a Web3-native agency, and our team works inside smart contracts, verification flows and protocol design every day. So the technical depth was never the obstacle. It was the fun part.

Animating The Code Itself

The core of the work is website animations that visualise the code Maxy runs. We built them as lightweight JSON animations that show the protocol doing what the docs describe, the verification steps, the swap flows, the proof logic, rendered as motion that plays natively on the website, inside documentation, and across investor briefs and explanatory documents.

The same system serves three audiences at once. A first-time visitor sees an elegant product. A developer sees an accurate diagram. An investor sees a team that can explain exactly what they've built. One animation system, three jobs, no hand-waving.

An Ongoing Partnership

The collaboration is still running hot. We continue to develop Maxy's creative as the protocol grows, with the goal of managing the brand's socials end to end and giving Maxy its full marketing face: one system from website to feed.

Why It Matters

Maxy is the purest example of why our agency exists. Designers who don't understand smart contracts make crypto work that looks beautiful and says nothing. We got to do the opposite, taking real cryptography and making it legible. Not just as marketing, but as a step toward a financial world where everyone can understand the technology their money runs on.

That's the work we want to be doing. And with Maxy, we get to do it every day.
